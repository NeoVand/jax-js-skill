jax-js skill — runnable examples

Two working models, both training in this tab on your own GPU. They exist to prove the skill's templates run, and to be read.

  • 1 · The approximator
    A small MLP fits a sine wave on the main thread. Pytree params, valueAndGrad, optax Adam, a jitted step, a canvas. Runs anywhere — WebGPU, wasm, or CPU.
  • 2 · The scribe
    A 2-layer character transformer, trained in a Web Worker, with a second worker writing samples from couriered checkpoints so the loss curve never pauses. Needs WebGPU.
